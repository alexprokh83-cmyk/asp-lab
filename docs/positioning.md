# ASP Lab — brand, positioning, wording (agreed baseline)

**Name:** ASP Lab. **Full name:** ASP Lab - Automation of Systems & Processes
(ru: Автоматизация систем и процессов; uk: Автоматизація систем і процесів).
In the interface and documentation always "ASP Lab". Not used: AI Automation Lab, AI Laboratory, Automation System Process,
Automation Systems & Processes, Automation System of Process.

**Telegram:** ASP Lab Bot — https://t.me/ASP_Lab_Bot (`PUBLIC_TELEGRAM_URL`). Placements: hero, final CTA, mobile menu, /contact and
the contextual CTA band of every content page (solutions, local AI, audiences, projects, approach, about, case studies).
/privacy and 404 intentionally have none. `npm test` fails if a page links to a different bot or if the old brand reappears.

**Positioning:** ASP Lab is a laboratory and solution-development team for automating systems and processes with AI, n8n, API
integrations and automation tooling. The site is a showcase of what can be automated; projects are examples and evidence of
capability, not the structure of the site.

**Honest status vocabulary (no invented clients, sales, KPI, ROI or production status; no artificial downgrading):**
implemented and working · own project · research / developing · concept or potential direction.

**Solutions — 8 directions (working basis):** 1 Documents · 2 Requests, appeals and correspondence · 3 Voice and meetings ·
4 AI assistants and Telegram bots · 5 Knowledge bases and RAG · 6 Process automation and integrations ·
7 Test automation and QA · 8 AI evaluation and model selection.
Local / Private AI is a deployment option across directions, not a business direction.

**Process:** facts are confirmed with the owner before wording changes (gap → concrete question → facts → proposal).

**Logo:** the supplied hexagon mark with "ASP" (`public/logo/asp-lab-logo.png`, processed to a transparent 4x PNG from the owner's
165x171 px JPG; `asp-lab-mark.png` is the mark alone, used for favicon / apple-touch icon). Header, mobile menu and footer use it as is on light backgrounds (the artwork has navy parts, so on a dark
background use the `plate` option of `Logo.astro`). The social card
(`og-image.jpg`) is the logo on the supplied hero artwork. For print, large formats or a perfectly sharp header, provide a vector
(SVG) or a larger original. The logo contains "ASP" only; "Lab" and the full name appear in text (footer, About, titles).

**Projects section (agreed):** a showcase of real, working engineering projects (13), not a catalogue of experiments. Each project page:
subtitle, about, how it works (process steps), features, where it can be used, technologies, a prominent real result when one is stated.
Not used: "laboratory experiment", "draft", "prototype" for working solutions, "not documented", "no measurements", "what a pilot would require",
evidence/limitations/TODO sections. Directions of the catalogue = the 8 Solutions directions.
